COS30045 Data Visualisation Assignment 3 - OECD Health Statistics, 2015–2024

Does spending more on healthcare increase life expectancy?

This project explores the relationship between healthcare expenditure and life expectancy across 10 selected OECD countries from 2015 to 2024. The visualisations allow users to compare healthcare spending, life expectancy outcomes, and changes over time.

Healthcare Spending vs Life Expectancy

Explore whether countries that spend more on healthcare per person tend to have higher life expectancy.

Healthcare Spending Over Time

Compare how healthcare expenditure per person has changed across selected OECD countries from 2015 to 2024.

Life Expectancy Over Time

Explore how life expectancy has changed across selected OECD countries between 2015 and 2024.

What the Decade Shows

Healthcare Spending

Spending varies across countries

Healthcare expenditure per person differs considerably across the selected OECD countries. The visualisations allow these differences to be compared across countries and over time.

Life Expectancy

Health outcomes also vary

Life expectancy differs between the selected countries. Exploring these differences alongside healthcare expenditure helps users investigate the relationship between spending and health outcomes.

Overall

Exploring the relationship

By comparing healthcare spending and life expectancy together, the project investigates whether higher healthcare expenditure is associated with longer life expectancy across selected OECD countries.

Data Source: Life Expectancy & Health Expenditure and Financing

Countries shown: Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, Korea, New Zealand, United Kingdom and United States.
