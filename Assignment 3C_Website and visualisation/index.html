COS30045 Data Visualisation Assignment 3 - OECD Health Statistics, 2015–2024

Does increased spending for health services mean improved life expectancy?

This project looks into the link between healthcare expenditure and life expectancy in 10 selected OECD countries from 2015 to 2024. The visualisations allow users to compare healthcare spending, life expectancy and changes over time.
